1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & REAL-WORLD GROUND TRUTH
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This report consolidates findings from an exhaustive 5-pass architectural bug 
hunt on NexChat. Ground-truth issues reported by daily mobile users on Android 
Chrome (Lagos) were directly validated against the codebase:

1. DUTY Chat: Outgoing green bubbles cut 50% to 60% off the right screen edge.
   Text, timestamps, and checkmarks are clipped. Incoming white bubbles render OK.
2. @Daven Chat: Bubble edge, timestamp (08:47 AM), and checkmarks cut off right 
   viewport margin.
3. Chat List: Conversation list is completely frozen. Users can see Chronex AI, 
   DUTY, Home Alone, king Alex001, @Daven, demon alexander, and Werxcsz, but 
   CANNOT scroll down to reach Fire, Supa, Monolog, and Coach Roach. Stuck at 
   Werxcsz.
4. Home Alone Chat: Date pills render (23 Oct, 25 Oct, 27 Oct), but the messages 
   area is completely BLANK. Top banner displays: 
   "GHOST PROTOCOL ENGAGED - Disintegrating in 30s".
5. Home Alone Chat: Bottom bar with Stegano-Voice / Token Rain / Co-Doodle / Beam 
   is stuck visible and sits directly on top of the message input box, 
   compressing input field width to <40%.
6. Regression Loop: Previous patch used `overflow: hidden` on body to prevent 
   bubble overflow, which broke scrolling across the entire application. 

All 18 bugs identified below must be approved for a coordinated, holistic fix 
to prevent recurring regressions.
