PRIVACY POLICY

Effective Date: October 26, 2026 • Architecture v2.6

1. Information We Collect

NEXCHAT is engineered with privacy and high-resilience cryptography at its core. We only collect the minimal information necessary to deliver real-time communication services:

  • Account Information: When you register, we collect your email address, chosen username, and user UID generated via Firebase Authentication.
  • Messages & Media: Text messages, voice recordings, and media files are transmitted across encrypted transport layers (TLS 1.3) and stored with per-message access control rules.
  • Presence & Ephemeral Telemetry: Online presence, typing indicators, and call signaling are processed in memory and ephemeral realtime websockets.

2. Cryptographic Enclaves & Storage

NEXCHAT protects user communications through multi-tiered cryptographic layers:

  • Cipher Vaults & Ghost Protocols: Private zero-knowledge storage is unlocked solely by client-side PINs. Server backends cannot inspect unencrypted vault content without client authentication.
  • Randomized Initialization Vectors (IV): In client-encrypted payloads, a unique 96-bit cryptographic IV is generated per transmission via the standard Web Cryptography API (`crypto.getRandomValues`).
  • Cloud Datastores: Backend services (Cloud Firestore, Supabase Realtime, and Cloudinary media delivery) operate under strict access control rules and Row-Level Security (RLS) policies.

3. Device Permissions

NEXCHAT requests runtime device permissions strictly when you activate specific features:

  • Microphone: Required for recording voice notes, live audio spaces, and voice/video calling.
  • Camera: Required for CamShot short video reels, video calling, and QR device pairing.
  • Storage / Media: Required to attach photos, documents, and cache chat history locally.

Permissions can be revoked at any time through your operating system or browser settings.

4. Data Retention & User Rights

Under GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, and international privacy standards, you maintain absolute control over your personal data:

  • Ghost Protocol Timers: Self-destruct messages and ephemeral status stories automatically expire and are purged from database records upon expiration.
  • Right to Erasure (Account Deletion): You may request full account termination and data purge at any time in Settings or by contacting our security team.
  • Right to Data Portability: You may export your conversation logs and contacts directly from your device.
